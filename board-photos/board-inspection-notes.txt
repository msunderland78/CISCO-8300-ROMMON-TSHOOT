ROM devices (corrected after sharper IMG_1244/1246):
  U2900 and U2901: matching 16-lead wide-body packages immediately to the
  right of the coin-cell battery in IMG_1246. The earlier provisional
  U2800/U2801 labels were misread from the blurred initial photo.
  Both carry RW170 as their lower marking (not RN170); upper marking 3TA15.
  Official Micron public marking-decoder query returned:
    RW170 -> MT25QU256ABA8ESF-0SIT, SLC 256M X4 SOIC.
  Original JSON response saved as micron-RW170-decoder-result.json.
  Manufacturer datasheet: 256 Mbit / 32 MiB SPI NOR; 1.8 V nominal,
  1.7-2.0 V operating range; 16-pin SOP2/SO16 wide-body package.
  In-circuit JEDEC ID and actual supply voltage have not yet been measured.
  Primary/golden assignment remains UNCONFIRMED. Matching capacities agree
  with flashmon's 32 MiB device boundaries. Read and preserve complete dumps
  from both devices before writing; retain board manufacturing/NVRAM data.
  Programming requires verified 1.8 V power AND logic signaling compatibility.

TPM physically identified:
  U5600, Infineon SLB9672, package marking AU20 31 / 00H2330.
  This is an SPI TPM 2.0 device. Supported by manufacturer's SLB9672 data.
  Physical identity does not establish whether the current boot error is an
  electrical fault, TPM state/firmware incompatibility, or Cisco NV provisioning.
  No TPM clearing, replacement, reset-pin manipulation or provisioning done.

Potential flash routing / selection:
  U2903 and U2904 markings PI3CH / 480QE / ZBY6G.
  Manufacturer confirms PI3CH480QE is a 4-channel 2:1 signal mux/demux.
  Datasheet: pin 1 Select (S), pin 15 active-low Enable, pin 8 GND, pin 16 VCC.
  Their placement beside the flashes suggests possible flash routing, but
  PCB net connections and actual function are NOT established by the photo.
  J2900 is a populated 2-pin header by the board corner; J2901 is a nearby
  populated 2x4 header. J2900 is a candidate selector/control connection only.
  Do not bridge J2900 based solely on its reference number or proximity.
  Verify ground, supply and continuity to mux select/enable nets first.

Headers and switches:
  Numerous populated headers are present, including J4400 (20 pins visible
  in IMG_1243), plus smaller headers around the battery and likely flashes.
  No ROM0/ROM1/GOLDEN selector has been positively identified. Header pinout
  and voltage are unknown; pin count/appearance alone is insufficient.
  Underside SW6000 is an unpopulated switch footprint in IMG_1237. Its
  function is unconfirmed and is not evidence of a recovery-ROM selector.

User multimeter observations (Fluke 111, resistance mode):
  J2900 across two pins: approximately 4.49 kOhm.
  J2900 pin farther from battery -> USB port metal shell: approximately 2 Ohm.
  J2900 pin nearer battery -> USB port metal shell: approximately 4.9 kOhm.
  Inference: farther pin is likely shield/ground, nearer is a resistor-connected
  signal/control candidate. USB shield-to-circuit-ground identity is not yet
  independently verified. These readings do not prove a ROM bank selector.
  Next proposed measurement: nearer J2900 pin -> U2903 PI3CH480 pin 1 (Select).

Further user measurements, Fluke 111:
  J2900 farther-from-battery pin -> U2903 pin 8 (GND): 0.2 Ohm.
  J2900 nearer-battery pin -> U2903 pin 1 (S): approximately 6.5 kOhm.
  User reports probe tips are large relative to the IC leads and contact is
  uncertain. Ground connection is supported; direct Select connection is not
  demonstrated. Do not infer that J2900 is a ROM bank selector.
  Proposed next test uses only large J2900 header pins: DC voltage across
  header, after normal board mounting/cooling and coin-cell restoration,
  with jumper absent. Meter must be in DC voltage mode before applying power.

Further equipment/access constraints and measurements:
  Board mounts upside down in chassis. User removed it for unpowered tests.
  User has only ordinary pointed Fluke probes; no insulated grabber leads.
  Powered header measurement has NOT been performed; do not power bare board
  without restored mounting/cooling.
  J2900 nearer-battery pin -> J2901 eight header pins: user reports OL for
  all except the bottom-left and bottom-right pins, which measure in kOhms
