TOOLS TODAY
CH341A + 1.8 V adapter: identify actual revisions, input/output orientation,
and level-translation components before connecting a router. A regulator
alone is insufficient. Measure target-side VCC relative to target-side GND;
Micron operating range is 1.7-2.0 V. Check accessible static high outputs as
well. A multimeter cannot prove high-frequency signal amplitudes/overshoot;
use the adapter circuit/part specifications to establish level translation.

8-pin clip: does not fit the identified 16-pin Micron packages. Do not clamp
half a package, straddle arbitrary leads, or assume an unrelated 8-pin device
is boot firmware. An 8-pin device may be read only after separate identification
and voltage/pinout checks.

Dupont wires: useful on accessible mapped headers and tomorrow's breakout.
Check connector genders and fit; do not force them. Use the shortest available
signal path, avoid added extensions, and route a ground alongside signals.
20 cm cables plus clip ribbon can impair SPI signal quality.

Needle probes: useful for unpowered continuity to the IC leads.
Hook clips: useful on sufficiently large, securely isolated header/test points.
Check actual hook jaw size; a 4 mm connector specification does not establish
safe clearance between 1.27 mm IC leads. Do not use oversized hooks on IC legs.

C8375 UNPOWERED HEADER MAPPING
Source photo: c8375-recovery/board-photos/IMG_1260-spi-pair-inspection.jpg
U2_CSPI is left, U1_CSPI right IN THAT PHOTO. Headers P1_CSPI/P2_CSPI and
nearby muxes are visible. No header pinout or bank assignment is verified.
Header names/placement do not establish direct access to either chip.

Before resistance measurements:
- Disconnect ALL router power inputs, USB, console, and other external cables.
- Photograph coin-cell orientation; remove it for unpowered board mapping if
  accessible without damage. Confirm no residual DC voltage on the measured
  nodes before switching to resistance. Wait for stored charge to discharge;
  do not short rails or capacitors to discharge them.
- Black lead COM, red lead V/ohm, never the current jack. Use ohms for numbers.
- Touch probes together and record baseline lead resistance.
- Establish exact package pin 1 from a clear top view and the data sheet.
- Measure header pins to verified flash VSS first. Record actual values.
- Then map each required flash signal to each header pin, separately for
  each flash. Photograph/number headers consistently before recording.
- Near lead-resistance values support a direct connection. Higher resistance
  can reflect a series resistor or another path; beeps alone do not prove a
  connection, and kOhms are not proof of a direct wire.
- An unpowered mux may disconnect legitimate paths; lack of continuity is
  not evidence that a chip or trace has failed. Do not bridge unknown headers.

C8300 EXTERNAL BASELINE - CAN DO BEFORE DELIVERY
1. Record full PID suffix/serial and how long STATUS stays steadily red.
   Cisco lists red as booting, blinking red as hardware-integrity error.
2. Use the actual labeled RJ45 CONSOLE port, not Ethernet or AUX. Physically
   unplug native USB console at the router, since it disables RJ45 access.
3. Use a known-good Cisco RS-232 console cable. Verify it on another working
   device if available; adapter enumeration alone does not validate the link.
4. Start logging BEFORE a cold start. Use 9600 8N1, no software or hardware
   flow control (different from the known G2 115200 setting).
5. Record console-active LEDs and STATUS/ENV/PWR at startup, 1, 5 and 10 min.
   Ten minutes is an observation checkpoint, not a definitive death threshold.
   Allow a longer observed run if safe/appropriate; stop for abnormal heat,
   smell, critical environmental indication, or repeated fault cycling.
6. If silent, try 115200 and inspect for any bytes; a second startup at that
   rate may be useful to capture an early banner. Other supported baud rates
   can be checked if there is still no readable output. Avoid endless resets.
   Leave at least 30 seconds off between controlled cold starts.
7. Test native USB separately with a known data cable; confirm a NEW macOS
   serial device appears and USB console-active LED. A serial USB device
   appearing proves the interface enumerated, not that the CPU booted.
8. If still silent on both validated paths: power off/unplug, inspect/photo
   logic board, DIMMs, connectors and flash markings. No G2 flash image applies.
   Confirm installed memory and the model's service procedure before reseating
   or trying supported known-good RAM. Remove optional modules only as a later
   controlled, documented isolation test with the specified filler/cooling.
9. Identify candidate C8300 boot flash before choosing a clip or voltage.
   If SOIC8, today's clip may fit; that does not establish firmware type,
   successful in-circuit access, or availability of a valid restoration image.
   Capture originals before any write. No mains-side PSU probing.
