This is an exact, unmodified 32 MiB read of the original active SPI flash.
Two independent device reads matched; USB-to-Mac copies also matched.
Source identified by Linux as /dev/mtd0 (spi0.0), 33,554,432 bytes.
Original firmware: SDK112308-UEFI-ROMMON-SoC2-v9.2.1-Release, development.

This full chip dump is NOT a flashmon upgrade package. It contains unique
board data and environment; keep it private and use only for this chassis.
The physical chip/bank mapping must be established from the actual board.
Do not confuse spi0.1 with a golden ROMMON bank.

Before external programming, identify the exact physical chip/package and
verify programmer voltage, signal levels and pin mapping against its datasheet.
Remove router power, console and USB connections before attaching a programmer.
Read the actual chip twice and preserve both original programmer dumps first.
Compare those dumps with the saved images to establish the correct target.
Only then restore the original chip contents and verify a complete readback.

The saved dump was taken while manual ROMMON boot (0x1820) was selected.
It may stop at a ROMMON prompt after restoration. Known prior working settings:

 BOOT=bootflash:packages.conf,12;
 normal automatic boot register=0x1822 (115200 console)
Set/sync those through ROMMON after restoration if needed; do not substitute
an arbitrary generic configuration register.

The newer 17.18(3r).s2.cp firmware was programmed and verified but did not boot
on this board: no console startup, blinking red status, fans maximum after cold
power cycle. Do not reflash that image as the next recovery step.
